Strata Studio — turn real places into layered generative art

Strata Studio is a free browser tool that turns real geography into poster-scale generative line art. Frame any area on Earth and it renders the terrain and street network of that exact place as layered contours, ridgelines, waveforms, and flow fields.

The inputs are real data, not textures: elevation from terrain tiles, plus optional buildings, roads, and water from OpenStreetMap. Search for a place or drag the map to frame a composition, then shape the result with studio styles, palettes, seeds, line density, feature influence, and animation controls. Everything renders locally in your browser — no account, no upload.

Classic styles include Contour, Ridge, Flow, Blueprint, Woodcut, Drift, and Signal. Experimental styles push the same data further with Seismic, Melt Map, Gravity Well, Magnetic Field, Pulse Rings, Terrain Sonogram, Building Interference, and Noise Amplifier.

Export a finished piece as a PNG up to 3000 px on the long edge (250 DPI print), as an SVG that pen plotters and print shops can use directly, or as a short animated GIF, APNG, or WEBM loop. Each composition is encoded in the page URL, so a result you like can be shared or reopened exactly as rendered.
